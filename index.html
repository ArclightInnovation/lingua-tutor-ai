🧩 Grammar Reference

Open a topic for a quick rule and practical Spanish example.

Noun Gender
Nouns are generally masculine or feminine. Endings in -o are often masculine and -a often feminine, but exceptions exist. Example: el libro (the book), la mesa (the table).
Verb Conjugation
The verb ending changes with the subject. For regular -ar verbs in the present tense: yo hablo, tú hablas, él/ella habla, nosotros hablamos, ellos hablan.
Articles
Definite articles are el, la, los, las; indefinite articles are un, una, unos, unas. They agree with the noun's gender and number: las casas, un café.
Word Order
The usual order is subject–verb–object, though the subject is often omitted when the verb makes it clear. Adjectives commonly follow nouns: (Yo) quiero una camisa azul.
